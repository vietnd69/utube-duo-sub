# Privacy policy

YouTube Duo Sub does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

- **No tracking or analytics.** The extension contains no analytics, telemetry or remote code.
- **Network requests.** The only requests it makes go to YouTube's own caption endpoint (`/api/timedtext`), reusing the parameters of the caption request the YouTube player has already made for the video you are watching. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party.
- **Stored data.** Your settings (on/off, secondary track mode, preferred language, font size and position) are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`, so Chrome may sync them across your signed-in browsers. Nothing else is stored.
- **Permissions.** The extension requests only the `storage` permission, and runs only on `youtube.com`, `m.youtube.com` and `youtube-nocookie.com`.

Removing the extension deletes its settings.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/vietnd69/utube-duo-sub/issues>.
